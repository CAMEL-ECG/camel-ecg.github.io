Capabilities

What CAMEL is built to do.

Forecasting

Predict arrhythmias minutes before they occur, grounded in statistics computed directly from the ECG.

Long-horizon context

Reason over seconds to hours of multi-lead ECG — not just 10-second snippets. Up to 10,000 seconds of signal per prompt.

Multi-ECG comparison

Compare signals across time, leads, or patients in a single prompt — enabling before/after and cohort reasoning.

Evidence-grounded reasoning

Every prediction cites specific beat-level metrics — RR, PR, QRS, PAC count, RMSSD — so outputs are auditable, not opaque.

Example output

A reading that cites itself.

CAMEL's forecasts reference specific beat-level metrics it computes directly from the strip — so clinicians can verify what it saw.

How it works

Signals become language, once they're tokenized.

A CNN encoder quantizes 1-second ECG segments into tokens that live in the same sequence as text. The language model does the rest.

Tokenize the signal

A trainable CNN encoder converts 1-second, 256 Hz, single-lead ECG segments into 64-dimensional latents.

Interleave with text

Signal tokens are projected into a 2560-d space and interleaved with text tokens inside MedGemma-4B, adapted with rank-128 LoRA.

Generate a grounded forecast

A lead-aware attention mask lets CAMEL reason across leads at each timestamp and produce a clinically cited forecast.
