FAQ

Straight answers.

Is CAMEL available for clinical use?

No. CAMEL is a research model and is not cleared for clinical decision-making. We are actively designing deployment studies with clinical partners — that work is on the waitlist roadmap.

Can I self-host the model?

Yes. Weights and inference code are public. See the Demo page for a ten-line snippet on GitHub and Hugging Face.

How often will you email me?

At most once a month, typically less. We email when we ship a new checkpoint, a new benchmark result, or an opportunity to collaborate.

How is my data handled?

We only store the email address you provide and, if you give it, your role. We don't set cookies on this site, we don't run analytics, and we don't share your email with third parties.

I'm part of a health system — can we partner?

Yes — that's a major reason the waitlist exists. Mention it in the role dropdown and we'll reach out directly.
